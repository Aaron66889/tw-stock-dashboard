Bug fixed
Previous score term:
  dist = (l1 - px) / px * 100
  price term = clamp(-dist * 8, -20, 20)
When px was ABOVE l1, dist was negative and -dist was positive. Therefore being farther above the buy point earned MORE score. As price fell into layer 1, that bonus disappeared, so score fell exactly when the buy zone was reached.

New price-fit term
- Above layer-1 zone: priceFit = clamp(14 - aboveZonePct*8, -8, 14)
- Inside layer-1 zone: priceFit = 14
- Below layer-1 zone: priceFit = clamp(14 - belowZonePct*4, 8, 14)
This makes score improve monotonically as price approaches the first zone from above.
