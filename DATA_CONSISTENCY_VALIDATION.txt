V16.8.47-DATA-CONSISTENCY
===============================
Only data-consistency fixes; model parameters are unchanged.

1) ETF quote ownership
- liveEtf4() now exposes last / prevClose / open / high / low / volume.
- buyModel() directly consumes liveEtf4() for 0050/0056/00878/00919.
- The price shown on the page and the price/open used by modelOne() now come from the same ETF quote owner.
- dataFresh requires both market context and all four ETF quotes to be fresh and complete.
- Fixes the front-end typo lastBuy.results -> lastBuy.models.

2) Breadth official-close lock
- During the session breadth remains ~30-second live-derived.
- Once same-day official TWSE MI_INDEX breadth is obtained after close, it is locked for that day.
- The 30-second background scheduler stops requesting breadth after the official lock.
- Lock clears automatically on the next Taiwan calendar day.

3) Browser stale-breadth fallback
- If /api/context fails, same-day official breadth may still display.
- live-derived breadth fallback is allowed only if same-day and <= 2 minutes old.
- Prior-day breadth is never resurrected by localStorage.

Unchanged
- First/second/third buy-point formulas
- Three-layer coherent re-anchor
- 09:00 open-price anti-chase ceiling
- Score / gate / chaseRisk parameters
- 08:45 TXF lead
- Manual/model trade tracking
- Holdings / Supabase / constituent logic / night futures
