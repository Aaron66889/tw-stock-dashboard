16.8.64-HISTORICAL-CHASE-CALIBRATION

Scope
- Only the four ETF anti-chase cutoff layer is changed.
- Layer-1/2/3 price formulas are unchanged.
- Hard Gate for stale data / knife-fall is unchanged.
- Constituents, market/ETF quotes, holdings, commentary and Supabase sync are unchanged.

Calibration
- ETF-specific history observations; candidates 80..95.
- Walk-forward: previous 3 calendar years train, next calendar year test.
- Live hard cutoff anchors to the median of up to the latest 5 walk-forward selected thresholds.
- Candidate cutoff must preserve >=85% chase-observation participation (blocked <=15%).
- Warning cutoff is informational; it does not veto a buy.
- Strong-bull / weak trend adjustment is limited to +/-1 and bounded by the historically stable band.
- Only validated TWSE backtest-ready history may activate calibrated cutoffs. Until validation/history warm completes, legacy hard cutoff 88 is used as a safe fallback.

No double penalty
- The hard anti-chase cutoff applies only while live price remains above the current cash-session open (or before a valid cash open exists).
- Once price returns to/below the session open / first-layer region, chaseRisk remains in the score but is not used again as a veto.
