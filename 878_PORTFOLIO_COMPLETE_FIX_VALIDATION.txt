V16.8.59-878-PORTFOLIO-COMPLETE-FIX
====================================

Scope: 00878 constituent/holding completeness only.

ROOT CAUSE
- 00878 tracks a 30-name index, but the ETF's actual portfolio can temporarily be
  29 stock holdings + a Taiwan index futures position.
- The prior code hard-required 30 stock rows and deliberately rejected futures,
  so a valid official portfolio was permanently shown as 29/30 and could not
  enter constituent-health scoring.
- Current public 2026-09-07 holdings are consistent with this structure:
  29 stock holdings (~97.37%) + 1 Taiwan index futures position (~2.75%).

FIX
- Keep stock parser strict: only ordinary Taiwan stock rows enter stock health.
- Detect official futures/hedge rows separately; they never enter stock quote or
  health calculations.
- Treat the official portfolio as complete when:
  * stock rows are near the nominal 30-name target,
  * recognized futures/hedge rows bring official positions to >= 30, and
  * combined official portfolio weight is within 95%~105%.
- In that valid 29-stock + futures case, stock-health source coverage denominator
  becomes the actual 29 stock rows, so 29/29 is complete rather than false 29/30.
- UI explicitly shows: official holdings complete / 29 stocks + 1 futures;
  futures are excluded from stock-health score.
- Parser now starts below the real holdings header, so fund-title/NAV/date rows no
  longer pollute rejected-row diagnostics.
- Constituent/health caches bumped to r337.
